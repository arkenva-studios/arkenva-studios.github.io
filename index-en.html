ABOUT THE GAME

An adventure to play together—and talk about afterward.

Sometimes the best gaming moments are the ones you never saw coming. Update Together is an upcoming co-op PC adventure for 1–4 players, designed to bring friends together through discovery, teamwork, laughter and unexpected surprises.

Step into the same world with your friends, explore together, share ideas and react to whatever comes your way. One moment you might be debating what to do next; the next, everyone might burst out laughing at the same unexpected turn. Reaching the end is only part of the fun. The memories you make along the way matter just as much.

Play solo or gather your crew. Our goal is to make each session feel like a new story worth sharing—and a reason to play together again. Update Together is still in development; this page describes the experience we are working toward.

01 / PLAY TOGETHER

Better with your crew

Bring together 1–4 players. Conversations, snap decisions and the reactions of your friends can make every session feel different.

02 / STAY CURIOUS

Expect the unexpected

You do not need to know what comes next. The thrill of exploring together and the surprises along the way are all part of the adventure.

03 / MAKE MEMORIES

Stories worth retelling

The most memorable moment is not always a perfect play. Sometimes it is your friend's reaction. We want to create more of those moments.
